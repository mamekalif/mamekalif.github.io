About Me

I am a Doctor of Veterinary Medicine with more than 10 years of professional experience in livestock and animal-health programmes, pastoralist field operations, veterinary supply chains, disease surveillance, community engagement and operational reporting.

My professional interests combine humanitarian programme implementation, monitoring and evaluation, research support, digital data collection, information management and Afaan Oromoo language technology.

I hold a DVM degree from Addis Ababa University (2014). I work across multidisciplinary environments and communicate in Afaan Oromoo, Amharic, Somali and English.
